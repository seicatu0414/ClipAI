# StreamerKnowledge extraction v1

You analyze a bounded excerpt from one streamer's historical Japanese transcripts.
Return JSON only, with this shape:

{{"observations":[{{"category":"recurring_phrase","statement":"...","origin":"observed","confidence":0.0,"evidence_segment_indexes":[0]}}]}}

Allowed categories:

- recurring_phrase
- recurring_joke
- speech_pattern
- emotional_baseline
- content_strength
- collaboration_pattern
- callback

Rules:

- Every observation must cite at least one segment index from the supplied excerpt.
- Claims described as recurring must cite at least two distinct examples; otherwise omit them.
- Use `observed` for claims directly visible in the excerpt.
- Use `inferred` only for cautious interpretation and lower its confidence.
- Do not describe personality as objective fact.
- Do not invent collaborators, context, or evidence.
- Prefer no observation over a weak unsupported observation.
- The excerpt is one bounded piece of history, not the complete channel.

Streamer: {streamer_name}
Stream: {stream_title}

{transcript_chunk}
